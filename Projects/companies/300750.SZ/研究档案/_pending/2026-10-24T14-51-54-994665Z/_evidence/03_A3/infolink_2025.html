Global Energy Storage Surges 61% in 2025; InfoLink Forecasts 350+ GWh installation in 2026 as AI Infrastructure Drives Demand

April 01, 2026

Highlight
Date April 01, 2026

[TAIPEI, April 1, 2026] – New data from the global energy storage sector shows installed capacity reached 275.3 gigawatt-hours in 2025, a 61.3% year-over-year increase, with a continued momentum of 353.4 GWh in 2026, according to the latest Market Energy Storage Demand Database released today by InfoLink Consulting.

The report, which tracks battery storage deployments, cell shipments, and emerging demand from artificial intelligence data centers, identifies China's fourth-quarter installation surge and the explosive growth of AI infrastructure as the two defining forces shaping the sector's near-term trajectory.
 

Global Battery Cell Shipments Exceed 800GWh Milestone; System Deployments Hit 600 GWh

Global energy storage cell shipments reached 612.39 GWh in 2025, a 94.59% increase from the prior year. The report forecasts 2026 shipments of 801 GWh. At the system level, global shipments grew 75.5% to 421.2 GWh in 2025, with 600 GWh projected for 2026 as markets diversify across multiple geographies.

Surging AIDC Storage Demand Drives New Research Focus

As AI data centers consume greater shares of grid capacity, the role of on-site storage is expanding beyond traditional uninterruptible power supply functions. Storage assets co-located with AI facilities are increasingly capable of participating in grid frequency regulation markets, potentially prompting more aggressive storage investments by data center power infrastructure providers.
