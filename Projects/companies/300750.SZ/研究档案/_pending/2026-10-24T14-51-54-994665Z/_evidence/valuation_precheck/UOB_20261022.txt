3Q26: Earnings Miss Due To Delayed Pass-through Of Tax Hikes  
Highlights 
• 3Q26 net profit missed expectations at Rmb21.25b (+14.5% yoy/- 5.8% qoq), 
as CATL bore all the export VAT hikes and 2% consumption tax. 
• We expect margins to recover in 4 Q26, as the tax rate hikes will be partly 
passed through to customers via higher battery prices. Having said that, the 
further roll-back of tax concession in 2027 will remain a drag on margins. 
• We cut our 2026- 28 net profit forecasts by 5%/10%/16% to 
Rmb89,121m/Rmb104,895m/Rmb116,370m respectively, as we now expect 
CATL to bear part of the tax hikes. 
• Maintain BUY and cut target prices for A- share from Rmb585 to Rmb343, 
and H-share from HK$675.00 to HK$400.00.

Analysis 
• 3Q26 results came in below estimates with GAAP net profit of 
Rmb21,246m (+14.5% yoy/- 5.8% qoq) and adjusted net profit of 
Rmb18,862m (+14.9% yoy/-9.8% qoq), vs our estimate of Rmb23.92b. This 
brings 9M26 net profit and adjusted net profit to Rmb64,530m (+31.6% yoy) 
and Rmb57,876m (+32.7% yoy) respectively, representing 69% of our full-
year estimates of Rmb93,637m (+29.7% yoy) and Rmb83,659m (+29.7% 
yoy). The earnings miss stemmed from margin contraction. 
- Battery shipment grew 44.4% yoy and 12.1% qoq to Rmb260 GWh in 
3Q26, in line with estimates,  including 195 GWh EV battery (+35.4% 
yoy/+12.1% qoq) and 65 GWh energy storage system (ESS) battery 
(+80.6% yoy/+12.1% qoq).

- Revenue grew 58.6% yoy and 11.8% qoq to Rmb165.22b in 3Q26, in 
line with estimates.  This takes 9M26 revenue to Rmb442.14b (+56.2% 
yoy), representing 69% of our full- year estimate of Rmb639.24b (+50.9% 
yoy). By comparison, 9M23/9M24/9M25 revenue constituted 
74%/72%/67% of full-year 2023/24/25 revenue.  
- Gross margin plunged 5.1ppt yoy and 2.5ppt qoq to 20.7% in 3Q26,  
and dropped 2.6ppt yoy to 22.7% in 9M26, vs our full-year 2026 assumption 
of 23.4%. The gross margin contraction in 3Q26 was due to China's cut to 
the lithium-ion battery (LIB) export VAT rebate and the imposition of the LIB 
consumption tax, as well as the time lag in the company's passing the 
relevant cost increases through to customers. 
- EBIT margin plummeted 5.0ppt yoy and 3.2ppt qoq to 13.3% in 3Q26,  
and dropped 2.0ppt yoy to 16.2% in 9M26, vs our full-year 2026 assumption 
of 17.0%. Other income/revenue ratio fell 0.5ppt yoy and hiked 0.2ppt qoq 
to 1.1%; taxes/revenue ratio soared 0.8ppt yoy and 0.9ppt qoq to 1.3%; 
SG&A/revenue ratio dropped 1.0ppt yoy and 0.1ppt qoq to 2.7%; 
R&D/revenue ratio fell 0.5ppt yoy and edged up 0.2ppt qoq to 4.3%. 
- Provision for asset write- down surged to Rmb1,712m (+8.4% 
yoy/+226.1% qoq) in 3Q26,  bigger than expected, due to the decline in 
lithium carbonate prices.  
- Operating cash flow plummeted 93.6% yoy and 94.7% qoq to 
Rmb1,409m, due to payable days shortening.  Trade payable days 
decreased to 134 days as of 30 Sep 26, from 158 days as of 30 Sep 25 and 
162 as of 30 Jun 26. CATL’s free cash flow turned negative at Rmb15.89b 
in 3Q26, from positive Rmb10.05b/Rmb13.88b in 3Q25/2Q26.   
• VAT cut and cons umption tax as the drag and overhang on earnings. 
Note that China cut the VAT rebate on LIB exports from 9% to 6% (ie VAT 
rate up from 4% to 7%) on 1 Apr 26, and began imposing a 2% consumption 
tax on LIBs on 1 Sep 26. It appears that CATL bore the additional costs and 
did not pass them on to customers in 2Q- 3Q26. Going forward, we expect 
CATL's margins to recover in 4Q26, as the tax rate hikes will be partly passed 
through to customers via higher battery prices. China's VAT rebate on the 
LIB exports will be cancelled by 1 Jan 27, and the consumption tax on LIBs 
will also rise to 4% by 1 Sep 27. That will probably weigh on CATL's margins, 
based on 2Q-3Q26 experiences. 
• The worry of market share loss is overdone, as CATL has formidable 
moats in technology, reliability and brand. CATL’s R&D expense reached 
Rmb22.1b in 2025, exceeding the combined R&D expense of Rmb13.3b for 
the second- to fifth-largest non- BYD players. This scale, patent lead, and 
quality gap make substitution costly. 
• Trim 2026-28 gross margin e stimates to 22.7%/22.1%/21.3%, from 
23.4%/23.7%/23.9% respectively,  to factor in the increased VAT and 
consumption tax. That is based on the assumption of a 50:50 split of the tax 
burden between CATL and its customers. 
Earnings Revision/Risk 
• We cut our 2026- 28 net profit forecasts by 5%/10%/16% to 
Rmb89,121m/Rmb104,895m/Rmb116,370m respectively.  
• Risks: a) slower-than-expected recovery of global EV sales, b) commodity 
cost hikes, and c) delay of battery price increases following cost hikes.  
Valuation/Recommendation 
• Maintain BUY and cut target prices for A-share from Rmb585 to Rmb343, 
and H-share from HK$675.00 to HK$400.00, based on a lower 2027F PE of 
15x (1SD below historical mean) and lower 2027F EPS. 
Share Price Catalyst 
• Catalysts: Margin recovery in 4Q26; the mass production of new products 
like the third-generation Shenxing Battery and third-generation Qilin Battery.
