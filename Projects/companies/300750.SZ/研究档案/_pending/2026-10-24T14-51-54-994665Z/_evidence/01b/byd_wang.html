Wang Chuanfu

Chairman and CEO of BYD Company Limited

Download pdf
Share this post on X Share this post on Facebook Share this post on Linkedin Share this post by mail

Mr. Wang Chuanfu was born in February 1966 in Wuwei City, Anhui Province, PRC, and is currently the chairman and president of BYD Co., Ltd., a senior engineer, and a science and technology expert. Wang Chuanfu graduated from the Central South University of Technology in 1987, majoring in metallurgical physical chemistry, with a bachelor's degree; in 1990, he graduated from Beijing Nonferrous Metals Research Institute with a Master of Engineering. Wang Chuanfu has served as engineer and deputy director of the Beijing General Research Institute of Nonferrous Metals, and general manager of Shenzhen Beagle Battery Co., Ltd. He founded Shenzhen BYD Industrial Co., Ltd. in February 1995.

 

Wang Chuanfu has adhered to the enterprise development concept of “Technology as Priority and Innovation as Foundation” since BYD was founded, relying on core technology, precise strategy and rapid decision-making mechanism, and is committed to building a leading technology and international brand with global competitiveness. Under the leadership of Wang Chuanfu, BYD shoulders the mission of “Satisfying People's Longing for a Better Life with Technological Innovation”, focusing on manufacturing and real economy for 28 years, adhering to the guidance of solving social problems and driven by technological innovation. It has continuously grown in the process of solving problems, and developed into a Fortune 500 enterprise spanning four major industries: automobile, rail transit, new energy and electronics, not only being the leader of China's new energy automobile industry, but also being committed to become the world's industry leader.

 

Wang Chuanfu was elected as a founding member of the “Sustainable Development Advisory Committee” of the United Nations Development Programme, and was honored as CCTV's Person of the Year in China's Economy, Individual Lifetime Achievement Award of “Zayed Future Energy Award”, Innovative and Entrepreneurial Personages and Advanced Model Personages in the 40th Anniversary of the Establishment of Shenzhen Special Economic Zone. Wang Chuanfu topped the List of China's 50 Most Influential Business Leaders in 2023 in the Fortune magazine.
